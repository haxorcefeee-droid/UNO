    House rules

    • Match the top card by color, number or symbol.
    • Skip ⊘ · Reverse ⇄ (acts as Skip in 1v1) · Draw Two +2.
    • Wild plays on anything — pick the next color. Wild Draw Four also makes your opponent draw 4.
    • No playable card? Draw one — if it plays, you may play it immediately.
    • Down to one card? Hit UNO! before your next turn or draw 2 as a penalty.
    • Room games: everyone posts the ante, the winner takes the pot (10% house cut).
    • Results are stored in a Neon Postgres cloud database.
